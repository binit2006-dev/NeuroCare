NeuroCare — Sample MRI Scans for Testing
=========================================

Place real sample MRI images in this folder (any .jpg or .png) and they
will appear as downloads on the website.

Suggested public datasets you can source MRI scans from:
  • Kaggle: "Brain Tumor Classification MRI" by Sartaj
  • Kaggle: "Brain MRI Images for Brain Tumor Detection" by Navoneel
  • Figshare: Brain tumor dataset (3064 MRI images)

Only use data you have permission to redistribute.
